Nerve Net Simulator

A jellyfish bell seen from above, with its web of nerve cells. Tap anywhere on the bell to stimulate the neuron there. There is no brain in the middle: every neuron only talks to its neighbours.

  1. Set touch strength below 50% and tap. The neuron twitches, but nothing spreads.
  2. Switch to chemical drift and tap. The bell crumples slowly and unevenly.
  3. Make a small cut, then tap. The signal flows around it. Cut across the middle and only one half squeezes.
