Prediction and Practice

Two short experiments from Part 5: catch your brain filling in what it predicts, and see why practice works best at the edge of what you can do.

  1. Close your left eye and look straight at the cross with your other eye. Keep looking at the cross the whole time.
  2. Move your head slowly towards and away from the screen. Start at about arm's length.
  3. At some distance, usually around three to four times the gap between the cross and the dot, the dot vanishes. It has landed on your blind spot.
  4. Now switch on a background and repeat. Where the dot disappears, you will not see a hole. Your brain fills in the line or the stripes it expects.
