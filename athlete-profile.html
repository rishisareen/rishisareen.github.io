The last three months

  • July: rehab. Strength only, 3–4 times a week, plus some yoga.
  • August: strength continued. Two illnesses (a viral bout, then H1N1) cost about 11 days. Zero cardio.
  • September: a deliberate restart with small sessions, none longer than 30 minutes. Swim 5 · bike 2 · run 3 · strength 7. About 12,000 steps a day.
  • October: around 5–6 short cardio sessions a week plus 3 strength sessions. Runs are 15–25 minutes, very easy.

Injury and health

June 2026: weak L4/L5 (disc bulge). Caused by prolonged sitting, bad posture and no mobility work, which led to anterior pelvic tilt and glute weakness that built up over time. It was not caused by a jump in training load. I withdrew from Hyrox and did rehab with a physio, who cleared me in August for easy swimming and cycling, with running restarting as walk-jog. Daily mobility and glute/core strength are now non-negotiable.

August 2026: a viral illness, then H1N1. Fully recovered.

Goals

Long term

  • A fast Ironman. The dream is sub-10.
  • Strong and healthy into my 70s, so strength and longevity matter as much as race times.

Now, Oct–Dec 2026

  • Rebuild the endurance base without injury, training every week, including travel weeks.
  • Get to 80 kg by mid-December.
  • Choose and enter a 2027 A-race.

What works for me

Works

  • Small steps, consistency first. Volume comes later.
  • A coach and accountability. I'm noticeably more consistent when someone is expecting me.
  • Clear numbers and structure. I'm data-driven and competitive.
  • Swimming as the entry point. Low impact, and I enjoy it.

Doesn't

  • Plans much bigger than my real capacity.
  • Training through illness.
  • Travel: the training mostly holds, but the eating doesn't.
  • Heavy work weeks, especially the week after a trip.

Practicalities

  • Weekday training windows: early morning, or 4–6 pm around work.
  • Some travel through October and November.
  • Delhi's winter air means mostly indoor training from November to February: trainer, pool, gym.

Questions for a coach

  1. Given where I am now, is sub-10 realistic, and on what timeline?
  2. Which 2027 A-race, and in which month?
  3. Zones: Garmin's "Zone 2" (149–164) sits right under my threshold, while MAF would put me at about 132. How should we set my aerobic threshold? Do I need a lab or field test?
  4. How do we build run volume safely after the back injury, and fit strength and mobility around it?
  5. How do I lose weight while building volume without hurting recovery? What fuelling do you recommend for long sessions?
  6. How do we handle Delhi's winter air and travel without losing the base?
  7. How you coach: check-in frequency, platform, and how plans adjust when life gets busy.
