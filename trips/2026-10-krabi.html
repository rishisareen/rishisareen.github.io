Good to know

Green season

Around 30 °C, with rain likely most days — usually a heavy burst in the afternoon rather than a washout. Mornings are the better half, which is why every boat leaves early.

Fewer people and greener islands, but the sea occasionally has other ideas. Boat days sit early in the week so there is room to move them.

On the water

Reef-safe sunscreen only — Thai marine parks turn away the other kind.

Jellyfish are more common in monsoon months, so we ask the crew before swimming at each stop.

National park fees are cash, collected at the pier, on top of the boat ticket.

Money

One baht is about ₹2.9 — near enough to multiply by three.

Cards work in the resort and in town, but boat crews, park rangers and beach restaurants are cash only, and nobody can break a 1,000 note.

Times are Thailand local, UTC+7 — ninety minutes ahead of Delhi. The status line and today's card read your device clock and update themselves.
