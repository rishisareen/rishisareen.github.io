Good to know

  • One suitcase each inside Japan

    JAL allows 20 kg each. The other suitcases go by courier: Tokyo hotel to Sapporo on 14 Dec, then Sapporo to Haneda on 21 Dec.

  • Buses to and from Niseko

    The Resort Liner runs from New Chitose airport to the Hilton door, and from the Hilton to Sapporo hotels. About 2.5 to 3 hours.

  • Real snow

    Niseko usually has 47–74 cm of snow on the ground in late December. Sapporo has 16–28 cm. Pack waterproof gloves and boots.

  • Short days

    In Hokkaido the sun rises around 07:00 and sets around 16:00. Do outdoor plans early.

  • Hot spring baths

    Wash and rinse at the seated showers first. No swimwear. Small towels stay out of the water.

  • If snow delays the 23rd

    JAL flies New Chitose to Haneda many times a day. The flight home isn't until 11:45 on the 24th, so there's a full spare evening.
