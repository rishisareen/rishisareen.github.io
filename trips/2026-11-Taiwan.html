    Set a card travel notice and get an eSIM by 10 Nov. Carry some NT$ from the first day.

Good to know

  • Entry

    The Travel Authorization Certificate is approved and valid to 27 Jan 2027. At immigration show your passport, the certificate, your US visa and the return ticket. You can stay up to 26 Nov.

  • No meat or animal products

    Don't bring any into Taiwan. The fines are heavy.

  • Bags

    Two checked bags of 23 kg, one 7 kg cabin bag and a small item. No power banks in checked bags. You carry your bags between cities.

  • Money

    Cash for night markets and small cafés. Cards work in hotels and bigger shops.

  • Power and transit

    110 V with two-pin Type A plugs. An EasyCard covers the MRT, buses and convenience stores.

  • Weather

    Taipei is about 19–25°C with some drizzle. The south is warmer and drier. Carry a compact umbrella.
