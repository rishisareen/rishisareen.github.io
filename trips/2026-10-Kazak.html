Day to day

Taxis

Yandex Go for everything. Minimum ₸400 (₹85), about ₸58 (₹12) a km — a hop across the centre is ₸400 (₹85)–800.

Never take a quote from someone who approaches you. That's the only scam here, and it lives at the airport.

Metro

One line, eleven stations, ₸120 (₹25), 06:20 to midnight. No cash at the turnstiles — Onay card ₸500 (₹105) or pay by app QR.

Almaly is your station at both flats. Zhibek Zholy for the bazaar.

Money

₸5 ≈ ₹1 — divide tenge by five. Visa and Mastercard nearly everywhere; pay by card by default. Keep ₸20,000–30,000 (₹4,150–6,200) cash for the bazaar, park gates and small cafés.

Draw it in one ATM withdrawal — Kaspi (yellow), Halyk (green) or ForteBank all take international cards. The flat fees make repeat visits expensive.

Rupees are not exchangeable here. Carry a little USD as backup and change it in the city, never at the airport. Decline dynamic currency conversion at every terminal.

Eating

Georgian restaurants are the reliable fallback if you want to eat without meat — khachapuri, lobio, aubergine with walnuts.

Indian if you need it: Eastern Gate (Shevchenko 75), Magic Masala (Abay 107a), NAMO for South Indian (Markova 38A).

Weather

Around 24 °C by day, 11 °C at night, rain on about six days across the whole month. Sunset drifts from 19:10 to 18:55 this week.

Talgar Pass at 3,200 m is a different climate. Fleece and a shell.

Paperwork

No visa — 14 days visa-free on an Indian passport. Carry both passports and photocopies.

Your host files an eQonaq arrival notice within three working days. The hotel does it silently; the apartment manager needs asking.
