This is the tenth edition of my monthly review. October was the first month of Q4, and the first month of living by the plan I wrote by the pool in Krabi.

If I had to sum it up in one line : **the body is coming back, the money is yet to move.**

October was my best training month of the year. 28 sessions in 31 days, and for the first time since I started running again, I ran without a limp. On the other side, my finance goal didn't move. Again. More on both below.

October was also the month plans kept changing ; especially on the travel. The Canada trip in December didn't work out with the kids' dates, so it's off for this year. Goa in late October didn't happen either : a family health matter came up that same week, and I was needed to be at  home. Now, the kids and I are going to **Japan in December**, and the flights were booked within ten days of Canada falling through. I'm quite happy with how quickly that came together.

## Family & Relationships

For the tenth month running, this was the strongest pillar.

**Ryan was with me for a good chunk of October.** We did normal things, and normal things turned out to be the best : walks around Cyber Hub (it's becoming our go-to place), burgers at McD, buying a big bag of crackers for Diwali, breakfast at a café. Three evenings we swam together in the outdoor pool, in very cold water. He only pushed me to go swimming (he is taking crazy genes from me!!!), we were both shivering at the start, and it was delightful. I also sat and watched him study on the computer for a while. He learns in his own way, and he's good at it. He is using AI so effectively ; tools like Google Gemini learning mode converts study into visual learning, and that is how it should be. 

For a few months now I've been trying to design a routine with Ryan : a weekly build session, a maths app, fixed calls. None of it has stuck, four months in a row. What works is just being together. So I'm done designing. I'll just count the days we spend together.

**Riya** came over from college whenever she could. We went to the arcade with Ryan, and on the last day of the month we watched *GoldenEye* together and she made pasta for dinner. A lazy, lovely evening. And yes, my baby got her first Tattoo!!!

**Friends** filled the month. I met Anubhav who started my whole Ironman journey back in 2016. Ten years! I walked and had lunches with a dear friend several times. I went to the half marathon expo with Rohin, and the next morning had breakfast with my group of friends after their race. Rohin ran a PB. It was lovely to be around people who are as driven about sport as I am. A whole afternoon in Lodhi Garden with another friend, eating pizza on the grass.

## Wellness & Fitness

This is the section I'm proudest of.

From Garmin and Hevy at the end of the month :

- **9 swims, 7 runs, 3 bike, 9 strength sessions : 28 sessions in 31 days**, plus 3 short bike rides and a hike
- In September it was 10 cardio and 7 strength. So I kept the momentum and built on it.
- **Steps : 310,268 for the month, an average of 10,009 a day.** On the last evening I walked to 18k to make sure the average crossed 10k :)

**The biggest win : no limp on any run.** In September I'd limp for the first couple of minutes of every run. On 9th October I ran 15 minutes without it for the first time, and it hasn't come back since. I'm doing run-walk intervals (4 minutes jog, 1 minute walk) and went from 3 rounds to 5 over the month. My jog pace improved by almost two minutes per km, and my cadence went from about 154 to 168. Small sessions, done regularly.

> My body can move. I am recovering, I am getting better.

Swimming improved too : pool pace went from 2:15 to 2:10 per 100m. And in the gym, the weights are going up on almost everything. Dumbbell bench went from 40 to 50kg.

I also went back to the physio mid-month. Two things are going on with my hips : an anterior pelvic tilt, and limited rotation. I have the exercises, and it's up to me to do them. After the session, my hips felt so relieved.

One small thing that made a big difference : **laying out my sports clothes the night before.** 

> It is interesting, after years of being consistent, still small things like reducing or removing friction works so wonderfully well.

Now the not-so-good part : **weight.** I started October at 87.2kg and wanted to be at 84 by the end of the month. I ended at 85.6. Progress, but slower than I wanted. And I know why. The training discipline is coming, more work needed on food aspect. There were a couple of lazy weekends of pizza and ice cream, and a few "just this once" meals out. I even asked myself in my journal if I'm bored of cooking and should get a cook. That's still an open question. 

Nevertheless, very happy to resume running this month. November is where I will continue to build on this. 

## Finance & Investing

Kept light, as always.

In my September review, I wrote that finance got zero slots on my morning list, and that was why nothing moved. So in October I put it on the list. It made "Today's 3" on five mornings.

And still nothing moved :)

Every one of those five mornings, the task said "research a fee-only adviser." And every time, it didn't start. Research has no end point. You can't finish it, so you never really start it.

Here's the part that stung when I saw it. In October I spoke to **three triathlon coaches**. I found them, messaged them, booked calls and asked good questions. That's exactly the same skill as finding a financial adviser. I did it three times for the thing I enjoy, and zero times for the thing I keep saying matters most.

So for November, the task isn't "research." That is vague. I will break it down into micro steps and execute it.

## Mind & Meditation

**Instagram came off my phone** in the second week of October. I don't miss it. I can't say it's made me dramatically calmer, but it's one less thing pulling at me.

The weekly plan happened every single week of October. That's now a solid habit.

There were a few heavy mornings. A couple of times I woke up at 3am with office work racing through my head. One morning I wrote about *"a fog of fatigue... mind feels scattered."* Another : *"one of those days where a million thoughts running."* And one line I think sums up a lot of my life right now :

> I've so many options of things to do ; challenge is pick and stick to one.

Two small practices helped. One day I wrote down every action as I did it. I'd read that saying what you're doing is a way to focus attention.

> That small act gives you a pause and awareness.

And in the last week, I made meditation the week's theme : just two minutes a day. Meditation has always been a stumbling block for me, so two minutes is the right size to start.
