Why care about mobility?

Low back pain

Lack of mobility causes you to round your lower back at the bottom of a squat, which loads the spine in an unsafe position.

Weak quads, knee pain

If your knees can't shift past your toes in a squat, you can't properly strengthen your quads. Weak quads mean weak knees.

Hip pain

The body is one full chain. What happens at the base makes other areas overwork.

Muscles left out

Without the range, you can't fully work the correct muscles during exercises. We are not getting any younger, so keep this up to stay fit.
