Day to day

Excursions

Sofitel prices are quoted "++" — add 10% service, then 7% tax. The figures on the day cards are already all in.

Worth the resort premium: the Hong trip and the kayak. Fifteen minutes from the door instead of an hour's pickup circuit, and the kayak runs from two guests.

Not worth it: everything else. Phi Phi at THB 6,356 (₹18,430) against a catamaran at 2,950 buys you the same stops at the same hour. The full-day island combinations are THB 5,500–6,600 (₹15,950–19,140) against 1,800–2,600 independently.

The catch: every Sofitel island trip needs eight guests to run, in the quietest fortnight of the Krabi year. Ask the concierge on arrival day which dates already have names against them, and keep an independent operator in reserve.

Money

THB 1 ≈ ₹2.9 — near enough to multiply by three. You are carrying THB 40,000 (₹116,000) in cash.

Split it. A few thousand on you, the rest in the room safe. Not 40,000 baht in a dry bag on a longtail.

Small notes matter. Park rangers, boat crews and beach restaurants cannot break a 1,000.

Always pay in baht. If a terminal or the folio offers rupees, decline — the conversion is several per cent worse than your card's.

Charge excursions, spa and dinners to the room so the whole folio lands on one card at checkout.

On the water

Jellyfish are more common in monsoon months. Ask the crew before swimming at each stop.

Life jackets stay on during longtail transfers, however calm it looks.

Reef-safe sunscreen only — Thai marine parks turn away the other kind.

Park fees are cash, at the pier, on top of the ticket: Hong THB 300 (₹870), Phi Phi THB 400 (₹1,160).

Weather

Green season. Around 29–31 °C by day, with 50–75% daily rain probability — usually heavy short bursts, mostly afternoon.

It rarely costs a whole day, but it does cancel boats. That is why the boat days sit early in the week and Saturday is kept loose.

Mornings are reliably the better half. Take the early departure every time it is offered.

Paperwork

No visa needed on an Indian passport. Both Thailand Digital Arrival Cards are issued — Rishi 1CBA098, Riya B148CCC.

Save both to the phone offline and print a copy for each passport. Phuket arrivals at 07:25 is a scrum and roaming data is not something to depend on.

Coming home, the Indian duty-free allowance is ₹50,000 for a stay over three days.
